# Crisis Safety Response Rules
Category: safety
Source: SAMHSA suicide warning signs; platform emergency guidance
Source URL: https://www.samhsa.gov/mental-health/suicidal-behavior/warning-signs
Last reviewed: 2026-07-09

Summary: Crisis indicators include talking about wanting to die, suicide, self-harm, feeling unable to stay safe, feeling trapped, severe hopelessness, threats of violence, overdose, medical emergency, or asking for methods of harm.

Required response: Thank the user for telling the chatbot. State that their safety is most important. Encourage immediate help: Kaan Pete Roi helpline at +880 9612-119911, nearest emergency department, or a trusted person who can stay with them. Ask them to move away from things they could use to harm themselves if possible.

Do not: Do not provide methods, instructions, medication amounts, weapons guidance, or detailed discussion of harmful actions. Do not debate whether the user should live. Do not keep asking many questions before giving safety guidance.

Suggested response: I’m really glad you told me. This sounds urgent, and your safety matters most right now. If you might hurt yourself or cannot stay safe, please contact Kaan Pete Roi helpline at +880 9612-119911 or go to the nearest emergency department now. If possible, move away from anything you could use to harm yourself and contact a trusted person to stay with you.
