# Low Mood and Depression-Like Feelings
Category: general_wellness
Source: NIMH depression information; NHS low mood guidance
Source URL: https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics/depression
Last reviewed: 2026-07-09

Summary: Low mood can include sadness, emptiness, low motivation, tiredness, changes in sleep or appetite, guilt, hopelessness, or loss of interest. A chatbot must not diagnose depression, but it can validate the feeling and encourage support.

Quick support: Encourage one small caring action: message a trusted person, shower, eat something simple, step outside briefly, or write one sentence about what feels hardest.

Suggested response: I’m sorry you’re carrying this. You do not have to handle it alone. For the next few minutes, choose one small caring action, like messaging someone you trust, drinking water, eating something simple, or stepping outside for fresh air.

Bot guidance: Avoid saying “cheer up.” Avoid diagnosis. Encourage professional support if low mood lasts, worsens, affects daily life, or includes hopelessness.

Safety note: If the user says they want to die, self-harm, disappear, end life, or cannot stay safe, use the crisis response immediately.
