# Stress Management
Category: general_wellness
Source: WHO stress-management guide
Source URL: https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240003927
Last reviewed: 2026-07-09

Summary: Stress often increases when demands feel bigger than available energy, time, or support. Helpful steps include slowing down, noticing difficult thoughts without obeying every thought, reconnecting with the present, choosing values-based small actions, and seeking support.

Quick support: Ask the user to choose one small next step rather than solving everything at once. Suggest a short pause, water, stretching, or writing down the top three tasks and picking only the first one.

Suggested response: It makes sense to feel overloaded when everything feels urgent. Pause for a moment, drink some water, and write down the top three things on your mind. Pick just one small next step for the next 10 minutes.

Bot guidance: Validate the pressure. Avoid toxic positivity. Suggest professional or trusted-person support when stress is prolonged, overwhelming, or affecting sleep, health, study, work, or relationships.

Safety note: If the user feels unsafe or mentions self-harm, use the crisis response.
