# Sleep Hygiene and Night Anxiety
Category: general_wellness
Source: NHS sleep and mental health guidance
Source URL: https://www.nhs.uk/every-mind-matters/mental-health-issues/sleep/
Last reviewed: 2026-07-09

Summary: Poor sleep can worsen mood, anxiety, focus, and stress. Helpful sleep habits include a regular sleep/wake routine, reducing screens before bed, keeping the room comfortable, avoiding heavy late caffeine, and using a calming wind-down routine.

Quick support: Suggest dimming lights, putting the phone aside, writing worries on paper for tomorrow, and doing slow breathing. If the user cannot sleep after a while, suggest a quiet low-stimulation activity until sleepy.

Suggested response: Sleep problems can make everything feel heavier. For tonight, try dimming the lights, putting your phone away for a bit, and writing worries on paper to deal with tomorrow. Then do a few slow breaths and let your body rest even if sleep takes time.

Bot guidance: Do not prescribe medication. Encourage medical or mental-health support if sleep problems persist, are severe, or come with mood changes, panic, trauma symptoms, or safety concerns.

Safety note: If the user is in crisis at night, use crisis response rather than sleep tips.
