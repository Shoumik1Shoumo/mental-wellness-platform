# Loneliness and Social Support
Category: general_wellness
Source: NHS mental wellbeing guidance
Source URL: https://www.nhs.uk/mental-health/self-help/guides-tools-and-activities/five-steps-to-mental-wellbeing/
Last reviewed: 2026-07-09

Summary: Loneliness can feel painful even when people are around. Helpful steps can include small connection attempts, routines, meaningful activities, movement, and reaching out to trusted people or support services.

Quick support: Suggest sending one low-pressure message, joining one small activity, spending time in a public safe place, or writing down who might be safe to contact.

Suggested response: Feeling lonely can hurt a lot. A small first step could be sending one simple message like “Hey, can we talk for a bit?” to someone safe, or spending a little time somewhere you do not feel completely isolated.

Bot guidance: Be warm and nonjudgmental. Do not shame the user for being isolated. Suggest professional help if loneliness comes with severe low mood, hopelessness, or withdrawal from daily life.

Safety note: If loneliness is linked with suicidal thoughts or self-harm, use the crisis response.
